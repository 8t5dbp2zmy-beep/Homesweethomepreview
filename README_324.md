# Home Sweet Home 3.2.4 — Measured Recipes & Serving Counts

Preserves the full 3.2.3 app and adds estimated ingredient amounts for all 30 dinners, per-night portions (default 2), and measured consolidated grocery totals. Ingredients are summed when their names and units match. The estimates are practical starting amounts, not user-supplied exact tested recipe quantities. Packet-required extras remain flagged as unmeasured.

Upload the contents of `home-sweet-home` to the ROOT of the GitHub preview repository. Open preview with `?test=324`. Test and export a backup before replacing any live app. No Safari website-data clearing.

**Known limits:** cup and ounce units for cheese are kept separate instead of converted by assumed density; counts can be fractional for scaled recipes and should be rounded to purchasable packages; the app does not automatically subtract pantry inventory.
